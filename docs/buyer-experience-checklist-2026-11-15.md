## Required end-to-end participant prototype
Buyer anonymously discovers three suitable cars; saves/compares them after sign-in; records a private concern; asks the relevant dealer; receives a persisted answer; requests an eligible test drive; observes pending then dealer-confirmed appointment. Dealer responds, manages a private follow-up task, confirms through the same calendar, and records a buyer-visible outcome separately from private notes. Repeat with declined/rescheduled/cancelled, stale eligibility, conflict, failed mail, unavailable listing and empty inventory. Engineering QA will use synthetic records. Actual customer validation is pending real participants and must never be inferred from these checks.
